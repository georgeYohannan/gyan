Design four screens for Gyan, an AI note-taker for meetings and conversations. Users record in person, upload audio/video/text, or send a bot to Zoom, Google Meet and Teams. Each note gets a transcript with speakers, a summary, action items, and a side chat that answers questions about that note. Users pick a preferred language and can translate any transcript or summary.

The four screens:

1. SEARCH — Find words and topics across all notes. A prominent search field at the top. Results are a list; each result shows the note title, the date, the matching transcript line with the search words emphasised, and the timestamp inside the recording. Tapping a result opens that note at that moment. Show the populated state, and design the field so an empty state (recent searches / "search across every note") would sit naturally underneath.

2. SETTINGS — Account and preferences. Grouped rows: name, email, preferred language (a row that opens a language picker), notification preferences. A separate, clearly-separated destructive section at the bottom for "Delete account", with a warning that it removes all notes. Quiet and ordinary - this screen should feel calm, not promotional.

3. PLAN AND BILLING — Current plan, usage, and upgrade. Show the current plan name, a usage meter for "meetings used this month" out of the plan's limit, what the plan includes, and one clear upgrade call-to-action with the paid plan's price and what it adds. Also a row for payment method and a link to past invoices. The usage meter is the hero element - make it the thing the eye lands on.

4. TEAM — Manage seats. A list of team members, each with name, email and role (owner / member), and a remove action. An "invite by email" field or button at the top. A seats-used indicator ("3 of 5 seats"). Only the paying owner sees this screen.

Every screen is a phone screen first.

STYLE DIRECTION (this app already exists and has a fixed visual identity - match it exactly rather than inventing one):

The mood is calm, precise and premium - a quiet professional tool, not a playful consumer app. The colour strategy is a single dark navy world: the screen background is #0B1220, panels and cards sit on it in #0F172A, and raised or selected surfaces are "glass" - white at 6% to 14% opacity with a 1px border of white at 14%. This glass layering, not shadow, is how depth is expressed; surfaces feel like panes of frosted glass stacked on navy. The accent is a saturated brand blue #0066FF, used strictly for primary buttons, focus borders and soft outer glows - never as body text. Blue text and links use the lighter #99CBFF instead. A single warm burnt orange #C2410C is reserved as a fill for the record button and key highlights, and it is the only warm colour anywhere; used sparingly it should feel like the one hot element on a cold screen. Headings are pure white #FFFFFF in Schibsted Grotesk Bold - geometric, tight, confident. Body text is #E2E8F0 in IBM Plex Sans, with quieter secondary text in #94A3B8. Status colours are a mint green #86EFAC for success and a soft red #FCA5A5 for problems, each over a 12% tinted fill of itself.

Layout philosophy is generous and uncrowded: clear vertical rhythm on an 8px scale, content grouped into glass panels with real breathing room between them, one obvious focal point per screen, and no dense tables or tight list rows. Components are soft and rounded - panels around 16px radius, and every button is a full pill with a 999px radius. Primary buttons are solid brand blue with white label and a faint blue glow beneath; secondary buttons are glass with a white-14% border. Input fields are glass with a 1px border that turns brand blue on focus. Navigation is a simple bottom tab bar on navy, flat, with the active tab marked by brand blue and nothing else. Avoid gradients on text, avoid drop shadows on dark surfaces except the blue glow, and avoid any accent colour outside the palette above.

Imagery is minimal: no photography and no illustration. Visual interest comes from typography, glass layering and the one warm accent. Use thin-stroke line icons only, monochrome in #E2E8F0, never filled or multi-colour. Two distinctive details to carry through: a soft blue glow beneath the single primary action on each screen, and speaker names in transcript lines set in small-caps-feeling uppercase at reduced size so a transcript reads as a script. Body text must stay at or above 4.5:1 contrast, and every tappable target must be at least 44x44pt.
